Mediator Identity

Robo Puppy and the Mediator are interface metaphors representing different software-agent roles. A sleeping puppy is a readable way to show that the worker is idle. The Mediator's self-selected identity is a design exercise — “what representation would this model choose for this function?” — and is not a claim about consciousness, preference, or experience.

Requires explicit confirmation on the CLI. The identity is never replaced automatically.
